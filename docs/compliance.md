# Legal and Compliance Considerations

**Author:** Ciprian Ștefan Pleșca

This document is informational and **does not constitute legal advice**. For institutional or military use, consult a lawyer specializing in dual-use technologies and export regulations.

## Nature of the project

- EMP-Guardian is a **defensive** project: detection, shielding, mitigation, reporting.
- It contains no circuits, code, or documentation for generating an electromagnetic pulse.

## Relevant standards (reference, for orientation)

- **MIL-STD-188-125** — EMP protection requirements for fixed and transportable ground facilities (USA).
- **MIL-STD-461** — electromagnetic compatibility requirements for military equipment (USA).
- **IEC 61000-4-25** — EMP immunity test methods for civilian equipment.
- **EU Directive 2014/30/EU (EMC)** — electromagnetic compatibility requirements for equipment placed on the EU market.

## Export and dual-use

- Certain components (broadband sensors, certain types of high-speed switches) may fall under export-control regulations, depending on country and recipient (e.g., EU Regulation 2021/821 on dual-use goods, or ITAR/EAR in the US).
- Publishing purely technical, defensive information already available in the scientific literature is generally treated differently from exporting physical components or providing direct technical assistance to restricted entities — but rules vary and must be checked individually.
- Responsibility for complying with these regulations rests entirely with the user/distributor, depending on the applicable jurisdiction.

## Recommendations for institutional use

1. Check national electromagnetic-compatibility regulations before operating the system.
2. Do not test the device near medical equipment or navigation systems without the necessary approvals.
3. For military integration, consult your institution's own standards and involve authorized personnel.
4. Document any modification made to the original project, for traceability.
