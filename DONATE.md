# Support EMP-Guardian

**Maintainer:** Ciprian Ștefan Pleșca — Independent Researcher, Romania

EMP-Guardian is developed and published **free of charge**, with no institutional funding, no company behind it, and no dedicated laboratory. It exists so that researchers, universities, and infrastructure operators without access to classified military standards or expensive proprietary solutions still have an open, reproducible starting point for electromagnetic-pulse protection.

The project remains, and will remain, **free to use, study, and modify** under the licenses described in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) and [`LICENSE-HARDWARE`](LICENSE-HARDWARE). Donations are entirely optional and do not unlock any additional feature, document, or access level — everything is already public.

## Why donations help

As stated openly in [`MANIFESTO.md`](MANIFESTO.md), this project's biggest current gap is **experimental validation**: real hardware, lab measurements, and calibrated test data. Donations go toward closing that gap — for example:

- Purchasing sensors, MCUs, and test components (see [`hardware/bom.csv`](hardware/bom.csv)).
- Access to EMC/EMI test equipment or lab time.
- PCB fabrication and enclosure prototyping.
- Time dedicated to documentation, testing, and community support.

## Other ways to support the project

Money is not the only useful contribution — and for a research project like this, it's often not even the most valuable one. See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) for how to contribute:

- Code, firmware ports, or bug fixes.
- Real lab measurements and calibration data (even partial).
- Documentation improvements or translations.
- Compliance/standards knowledge from civilian or military EMC/EMP domains (no classified information).

Thank you for supporting open, independent, and reproducible research.
